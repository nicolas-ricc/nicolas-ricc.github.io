## Decisions (technical assumptions)

- **Interior stroke width is 1.0 u, not 0.5.** At 0.5 the tube measures 0.5·1.17·√2 = 0.83 px CSS (vertical runs) and 0.5·1.17·1.4 = 0.82 px (horizontal runs). That is below the issue's acceptance criterion, "trazo interior ≥ 1.5 px a 1.17". The issue fixes the precedence: "El tamaño sale del criterio … no se achica". The minimum is 1.5 / (1.17·1.4) = 0.916 u. 1.0 u is the tube width the sibling signs use (story decisions 12 and 14) and gives 1.64 px / 1.65 px. On the 1.6 fill that leaves 0.3 u of opaque safety on each side, inside the 0.25 u officeDark outline, so the letter stays "medio neón". The PR states it as a deviation.
- **Interpenetration is checked in the world box or in the wall's frame.** The world AABB of the 42.86 u wall at 45° spans ±15.6 in x and y. Anything standing 2–4 u in front of the face (lamps z 0..5, drums, container) is inside that box and overlaps the wall in z, so the issue's literal world-box test cannot hold for the geometry the same issue fixes. A pair passes if its boxes are disjoint (x/y or z, tol 1e-6) **either** in world axes **or** in the wall frame (x_f = (x − y)/√2 along d, y_f = (x + y)/√2 along n; `sortByDepth`'s `heading` frame for −π/4). The cap × wall pair is excused, as the issue says. A separate test pins the painter order: `sortByDepth(out)` draws the wall before every prop that stands in front of it.
- **Coordinates.** k = 8.55/7, W = `measureText("Portfolio", 8.55).width` = 31k = 37.864, L = W + 5 = 42.864, t = 1.2, d = (1, −1)/√2, n = (1, 1)/√2, c = origin. A point on the face at distance `a` from the left end and height z is `D + d·a` at z, with D = −d·L/2 + n·t/2. The text (a, b) of the word maps to the plate point `{ x: (2.5 + a)/L, y: 1.5 + b }`. "m in front" means n·p − t/2 = m.
- **Plate order** on wall 0: joints (4), stains (4), shadow, outline, fill, drips (5), signature. Wear sits under the paint, and the drips and the signature go on top of it.
- **Joints**: `rect`, u0 0, u1 1, z0/z1 = zj ∓ 0.04 for zj ∈ {2.9, 5.8, 8.7, 10.9}, `concrete`, `toneOffset −1`.
- **Stains**: `disc`, `concrete`, `toneOffset −1`. Center (a, z) and size (width × height) in u: (6, 1.3) 2.6 × 1.8; (30, 1.1) 3 × 1.6; (4, 9.8) 2 × 1.5; (39, 6) 1.6 × 2.8. Fractions are a/L.
- **Drips**: `rect` `safety`, 0.25 wide, centered at `a = 2.5 + x·k`, where x is the bottom of the stem in the glyph's grid offset: P x 0, t x 13, f x 17, l x 24, final o x 29.5. z1 = 1.5, lengths 1.4, 1.0, 1.3, 1.1, 1.45. The bottoms stay ≥ 0.05, so they never go below the wall foot.
- **Signature**: `fillText("rust", 2.2, 0.5)` as `signal` poly plates. It ends 1 u before the right end: plate point `{ x: (L − 1 − 4.714 + a)/L, y: 0.6 + b }`, with 4.714 = `measureText("rust", 2.2).width`. It overlaps the lower bowl of the final "o". That is accepted and reported with the capture.
- **Floodlights**: `industrialLamp(p, { h: 5, center: true })` with p on the ground at s = (u − 0.5)·L along d, u ∈ {0.2, 0.5, 0.8}, 4 u in front of the face (m = 4). The `cyan` bulb dot of `industrialLamp` is kept, since it is the Portfolio lamp of the kit. **Beam**: one `poly` `amberBleed` alpha 0.12 per lamp, `[bulb − d·0.3, bulb + d·0.3, F(s + 4, z 9), F(s − 4, z 9)]`, with F a point on the face. The issue's "z 1..9" is read as the band the fan washes: from the bulb, which projects onto the face near z 3, up to z 9.
- **Drums**: 3 `cylinder` `rust` r 1.1 h 1.6 `sides: 6` at z 0, in front of the left end: (a 1.6, m 2.2), (a 4.2, m 2.2), (a 2.9, m 4.6). They are disjoint in the wall frame, and their tops (z 1.6) stay below the baseline on screen.
- **Container**: `container(v3(x0, y0, 0))` (6 × 2.4 × 2.4 `rust`, axis-aligned). Its back corner (x0, y0) is 2 u in front of the face (x0 + y0 = √2·2.6). Its left screen edge, x0 − y0 − 2.4, starts 0.5 past the signature's right end, √2·(L/2 − 1) + 0.5. That gives x0 − y0 = √2·(L/2 − 1) + 2.9. It therefore sits at the wall's right end and does not cover the word or the signature.
- **Cap**: `poly` `stone`, the same `[D, C, B, A]` construction with L + 0.3 and t + 0.3, z 11.5, h 0.3.

## Review Focus

- **Paint stays on the face.** Every plate point has u ∈ [0, 1] and z ∈ [0, 11.5], and nothing paints outside the wall. Pinned in Task 2.
- **Props do not hide the word.** The screen top of every drum and of the container lies below (greater y') the projected baseline at the same x', and the container's screen x-range does not overlap the word's or the signature's. Pinned in Task 3.
- **Painter order.** With the props inside the wall's world box, the tie-break decides. `sortByDepth(out)` must place the wall (and cap) before every drum, the container and every lamp post. Pinned in Task 3.
- **Neon only in front of the fill.** Every `amber` tube polygon is at n·p = t/2 + 0.06, and its points stay within the wall's u-range and its z-range. Pinned in Task 1.
- **Legibility arithmetic.** `xHeightPx(8.55, 1.17) ≥ 10`, and the interior stroke 1.0·1.17·1.4 ≥ 1.5 px. Pinned in Task 1.
