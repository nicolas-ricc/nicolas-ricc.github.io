# Barrios: classic → transition → modern gradient, catalogue per barrio, neighbour rule and the singular pieces (#477) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the west-bank parcels stop using the provisional map of #472 (`clasico → downtown`, `transicion → residential`, `moderno → district`) and get the final zoning instead. Each barrio gets its own style, catalogue, height cap and lights. A one-step neighbour rule applies, towers are allowed only in the northwest, parcel lots carry their polygon with chamfers (ochavas), and the singular pieces (civic palace, theatre, library, construction site, water tower) come back on named parcels.

**Architecture:** the zoning stays a pure module (`src/city/zoning.ts`). New pieces: `parcelNeighborhoods(ps)`, which assigns by distance and then applies the neighbour rule; `towerZone(p)`; `maxHFor(n, p)`; `parcelLot(...)`, which builds the lot with an inset polygon and ochavas; and `FIXED_PARCELS`, the singular pieces by `cell`. The kit gains four styles and a `poly` flag on the recipes that clip to `lot.poly`. `city.ts` puts the water tower back. `lights.ts` widens the animatable pool to the new barrios, and the lab and the docs follow.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest (node, no Pixi), agent-browser only for the screenshots.

**Spec:** issue #477 ("Qué construir", "Decisiones que hereda este hijo", "Criterios de aceptación" are binding); story #471 plan comment (D3, D11, D12, D15); `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-resume-centro-costero-design.md` §6 and §7.4.

## Technical assumptions (declared)

- **A1. Poly-aware recipes.** `RecipeDef` gains `poly?: true`, set on the twelve recipes that clip to `lot.poly`: `classic`, `chamfer`, `decoStepped`, `podiumTower`, `courtyardO`, `courtyardU`, `patioResidential`, `rowHouses` (the `POLY_AWARE` list of `kit.test.ts`/`porteno.test.ts`) and the four `porteno` ones. Only they get a polygon lot. Any other recipe (the landmarks, `glassTower`, `twinTowers`, `campus`, `shopRow`, `gallery`, `constructionSite`) gets `rect` minus `SIDEWALK`, which lies inside the parcel because `rect` is inscribed. The kit contract (#476 plan, "`Lot.poly`: … contained in the lot rectangle") is why the lot of a polygon parcel is the bounding box of the inset polygon and not the inscribed `rect`: with the inscribed rect the polygon would contain the lot and clip nothing.
- **A2. Ochavas.** Measured on the 32 built parcels × 10 seeds, with the criterion's oracle (a right corner of `parcel.poly`, moved `SIDEWALK` inward along both edges, and the triangle with legs 2 there free of ground-level plans): `courtyardO` fails on every rectangular lot, and `classic` and `cornerCupola` fail on one wedge each. The criterion "classic and transition parcels are chamfered" and spec §6.2 ("Ochavas en todas las esquinas del casco y de la transición") cannot both hold with "`Lot.poly` solo para parcelas con lado oblicuo" plus unchanged recipes. The resolution is that every non-fixed `clasico`/`transicion` parcel gets a `Lot.poly`: its lot polygon (the inset parcel polygon, or the lot rectangle when the parcel is rectangular) passed through `ochava(poly, k) = chamferPoly(chamferPoly(poly, k), 2)`, with `k` = 2.4 for clasico and 2 for transicion (spec §6.1 table). The second pass at 2 cuts the corners whose edges are too short for 2.4 (≤ 7.2), which is the `(73, 291)` case. Measured with this rule: 0 failures across the nine recipe × barrio pairs. The ochava is the only polygon that a rectangular parcel receives. Every other barrio follows "solo con lado oblicuo".
- **A3. Fixed caps.** "`LANDMARK_MAX_H` sigue para `FIXED`" and "towers only north of y 290: ninguna pieza con `h > 21` tiene `c.y ≥ 290` o `d < 205`" both bind the obra: its crane reaches `min(22, cap)` + jib. A fixed parcel outside the tower zone therefore gets `min(LANDMARK_MAX_H, 21)` = 21. The `h` of a piece is `max(bounds(s).max.z) − lot.z` over its solids.
- **A4. Library cell.** `(103, 201)` exists with the world grid, but it is a 24 × 8 wedge (inscribed rect) whose lot is 21 × 5 (`tooSmall`). Its bounding-box lot cannot hold the library either, because the library ignores `poly` (A1). The issue's fallback applies: "la pieza va a la parcela más cercana que mire a la plaza o al eje". That parcel is `(73, 201)`: centroid 31 u away, lot 21 × 13, open `s` onto the `plaza` axis band. A test states the fact (`(103, 201)`'s rect lot is `tooSmall`) and pins the choice: the nearest built parcel other than `(103, 201)` with a side open onto `PLAZA_MAYOR` or the `plaza` axis, with no size filter. The library needs wings of 5, a portico of 3 and a courtyard of 1 or more (`src/kit/forms/landmarks.ts:136-160`), so lot depth ≥ 9; 13 is enough.
- **A5. Fixed parcels and ochavas.** `FIXED_PARCELS` get no ochava and are exempt from the chamfer criterion, because they are singular pieces. The tank host keeps its courtyard (`patioResidential` on a plain rect lot) and the obra keeps its fence.
- **A6. Animatable windows.** `lights()` draws its 40 animatable windows from `downtown` and `residential`. After this issue no west-bank parcel is either, so the pool would empty. The pool becomes `downtown`, `residential`, `clasico`, `transicion`, `bajo`: the barrios that replace the old ones, plus `bajo` (the warm low-rise).
- **A7. rng streams.** `moderno` takes the place of `district` for parcels: it draws with `districtRng` and its `sign` slots become district signs (`city.ts`). The no-repeat retry of `residential` extends to the `clasico`, `transicion` and `bajo` parcels. With the current `CITY_BOUNDS` (x ≥ −54) no built parcel is `moderno` (the westmost, `(−47, 171)`, has `d` 204.1); the tower rules are still coded and tested, both on synthetic parcels and on `parcels(PLAN_BOUNDS)`.
- **A8. `manzana-distrito`.** No built parcel is `moderno` until #479 takes the city to the view's edge (A7), so a region exhibit cannot frame one. `manzana-distrito` becomes a `piece` exhibit: `glassTower` (ruled in stage 4: the kit caps the non-landmark `podiumTower` at `RECIPE_MAX_H` 18, the landmark `glassTower` at `LANDMARK_MAX_H` 24, so only the latter rises above 21) with `STYLES.moderno`, `createRng(SEED)` and cap 50, on the lot (`rect − SIDEWALK`) of the plan parcel `cell (−77, 201)`. That is the tower-zone parcel nearest the city (`d` 227, rectangular), translated so the lot is centred at the origin. #479 can turn it back into a region.
- **A9. Exhibit heights.** The issue's `h 14` (`manzana-casco`) and `h 20` (`manzana-transicion`) are starting values. The framing gate of `exhibits.test.ts` (smallest integer that covers the structure within `SLACK`) sets the final value, which is written down with its measurement as the other exhibits do. The same applies to `obra`.
- **A11. Neighbours across axes.** The issue gives both "distancia entre sus polígonos ≤ `STREET + 1`" and "Ejes y plazas no separan vecinos (una avenida entre dos parcelas no exime)". Axis bands are 8 to 20 wide (`AXES` in `src/scenes/city-plan.ts`), so the distance rule alone would let an avenue exempt two parcels. The two are reconciled by also counting as neighbours the parcels of adjacent grid cells (the 8-neighbourhood at `PITCH` 30) and the parcels of the same cell. With today's 11 clasico + 21 transicion parcels, neither definition finds a violation.
- **A12. Fixed non-civic parcels in the rule.** The obra and the tank host take part as subjects and as neighbours: the rule may change their neighbourhood (style and lights), never their recipe or their cap (A3). Today they stay `transicion`.
- **A13. Missing named cells.** `assign` throws `missing fixed parcel <col>,<row>` when an entry of `FIXED_PARCELS` matches no built parcel, and `city()` throws (as it did before #472) when the tank host has no `courtyard`. A Task 4 test asserts that every entry matches exactly one built parcel whose lot is not `tooSmall`.
- **A14. Re-measured literals.** Only measured values move (sliver count, exhibit `h`, budget comments), each with its figure in the comment. `city-budget.test.ts` caps follow the issue ("medidos y escritos (×1.1, centena) si cambian"). A cap is never raised to cover something not measured.
- **A10. Library facing.** The library's portico faces south (fixed in the recipe), which at `(73, 201)` is the `plaza` axis. `FIXED_PARCELS` carries no `open` override: `lot.open` is `parcelOpenSides(p)`, as for any parcel.
