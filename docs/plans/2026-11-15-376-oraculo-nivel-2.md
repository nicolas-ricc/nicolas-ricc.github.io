# Level-2 oracle, context exhibits and the Blog sign through §4.1–4.2 (#376) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A pure level-2 legibility oracle (`signSpec`, `signMetrics`, `staticContrast`, `backingMargin`, `occlusionRatio`, `checkSign`), a storybook exhibit kind `context` that adds a sign to a crop of the real world, and the Blog sign rebuilt from its `SignSpec` at `SIGN_FONT` with a dark backing panel, shown at `#plataforma-escena` and gated by `src/lab/signs.test.ts`.

**Architecture:** `src/kit/legibility.ts` grows from level 1 (font) to level 2 (projected sign). A sign publishes a `SignSpec`: its glyphs placed in the world, one plane per glyph, plus its own backing solids. It draws its accents from that spec, so what is measured is what is drawn. Depth tests use the camera ray `p + t·VIEW_DIR` against the plane of each `tessellate` face, never AABBs. `src/lab` gets a third `Exhibit` variant, `context`: `contextScene` = `cropScene` + the sign built whole in world coordinates. A single gate test runs `checkFont` + `checkSign` over every context exhibit.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest (`npm test`), `tsc` (`npm run typecheck`), Pixi v8 only in `storybook.ts`.

**Spec:** issue #376 ("Qué hacer", "Decisiones que hereda", "Criterios de aceptación"). Story context: #374 and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-carteles-de-zona-rediseno-design.md` §4.2, §5. Where they differ, the issue wins.

## Decisions (technical assumptions)

- **`SIGN_RULES`** takes the issue's literal: `{ [COVER_LAPTOP]: { xHeightPx: 12, strokePx: 2, airPx: 2, contrast: 4.5 }, [COVER_PHONE]: { xHeightPx: 8, strokePx: 1.5, airPx: 1.5, contrast: 3 }, baselineDeg: 3, facadeBaselineDeg: 27, verticalDeg: 4, aspectTol: 0.08, backingW: 1, occlusion: 0.05 } as const`. `checkSign` with a scale other than 1.17 or 0.83 throws `Error("legibility: no sign rules for scale <s>")`.
- **`signSpec`** maps glyph `i` with `glyphPlane?.(i, g) ?? plane`. `quads` are `layout.quads` for `style: "stroke"` (what `strokeText` draws) and `layout.fill` for `"fill"`, each point taken through `toWorld(plane, x, y)`. `counters[k] = { at: toWorld(plane, c.at.x, c.at.y), r: c.r }`. Spaces give no glyph (as `layoutText`).
- **Metrics** (`project` from `src/iso/project.ts`; screen px = projected · `scale`):
  - `xHeightPx`: min over glyphs of `|project(toWorld(P, 0, xH)) − project(toWorld(P, 0, 0))| · scale`, with `xH = GRID.xHeight · size / GRID.cap`.
  - `strokePx`: min over every quad of every glyph of its shortest projected edge · scale.
  - `airPx`: min over consecutive glyph pairs (`glyphs[i]`, `glyphs[i+1]`) of the min `polygonDistance` between their projected quads, · scale. With fewer than 2 glyphs, `Infinity`. `polygonDistance` (convex polygons, already in `lettering.ts`) gets exported.
  - `baselineDeg`: least-squares line `y = m·x + b` through `project(toWorld(P_i, left_i, 0))`, `|atan(m)|` in degrees. With fewer than 2 glyphs, 0. With all x equal, 90.
  - `verticalDeg`: max over glyphs of the angle between `project(origin + v) − project(origin)` and screen up `(0, −1)`, in degrees (`atan2(|dx|, −dy)`, so upside-down text gives > 90).
  - `aspectError`: max over glyphs of `|(H'/W') / (H/W) − 1|`. `W × H` is the flat ink `box`, and `W' × H'` is the screen AABB of the box's 4 corners taken through the glyph's plane.
- **Depth test.** For a face `F` (from `tessellate`, so `dot(F.normal, VIEW_DIR) > 0`) and a point `p`: `t = dot(F.normal, F.pts[0] − p) / dot(F.normal, VIEW_DIR)`. `F` covers `p` when `pointInPolygon(project(p).x, project(p).y, F.pts.flatMap(q => [project(q).x, project(q).y]))` (`src/map/geo.ts`). `F` is in front when `t > 1e-6` and behind when `t < 0`.
- **`backingMargin(spec, solids)`**: for each glyph, a 7 × 7 grid of points (edges included) over its ink `box` expanded by `SIGN_RULES.backingW · spec.width` on every side, in text space, taken to the glyph's plane. A point passes when (a) some face of `spec.backing.flatMap(tessellate)` covers it from behind, and (b) no face of `solids.flatMap(tessellate)` covers it from in front. Returns passed / total (1 when there are no glyphs).
- **`occlusionRatio(spec, solids)`**: samples are the 4 corners and the centroid of every quad of every glyph. A sample counts when some face of `solids.flatMap(tessellate)` covers it from in front. Returns covered / total (0 when there are no samples). The backing is behind the letters and drops out on its own, not by identity.
- **`staticContrast(spec)`**: per glyph, take the center of its ink box on its plane, and among the backing faces that cover it from behind, the nearest one (largest `t < 0`). Its color is `toneColor(face.mat, stepTone(face.tone, face.toneOffset))`. The glyph's contrast is `contrastRatio(ISO_COLORS[spec.color], that color)`. A glyph with no backing face behind its center scores 1. Return the min over glyphs (`Infinity` with no glyphs).
- **`relativeLuminance(hex)`**: WCAG 2.x sRGB (`c ≤ 0.04045 ? c/12.92 : ((c+0.055)/1.055)^2.4`, weights 0.2126/0.7152/0.0722). **`contrastRatio(a, b)`** = `(max + 0.05) / (min + 0.05)`, symmetric.
- **`checkSign` rule names** (`Violation.rule`): `xHeight`, `stroke`, `air`, `baseline`, `vertical`, `aspect`, `contrast`, `backing`, `occlusion`. `limit` is a readable string such as `"≥ 12 px"`, `"≤ 3°"`, `"≤ 27°"` (facade), `"≥ 4.5:1"`, `"= 1"`, `"≤ 0.05"`. `value` is the measured number.
- **Context exhibit type:** `{ kind: "context"; box: Box; h?: number; build: (out: Solid[], accents: Accent[]) => void; sign: SignSpec; animate?: (opts: { reducedMotion: boolean }) => Animator }`, exported as `ContextExhibit = Extract<Exhibit, { kind: "context" }>`.
- **`contextAnimators(animators, e, reducedMotion = false)`**: the issue writes it with two arguments. The third, defaulted argument passes the storybook's `reducedMotion` on to `e.animate`, as the piece branch already does.
- **Blog layout** (prototype measured at `SIGN_FONT`; the ink box of "Blog" runs from −0.675 to 22.746 in u and from −3.618 to 10.975 in v):
