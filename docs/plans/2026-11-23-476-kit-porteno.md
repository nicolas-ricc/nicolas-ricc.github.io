# Kit porteño (#476) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Lots can carry a parcel polygon (`Lot.poly`). Eight existing recipes clip and chamfer to it, and a new `porteno` family (`cornerCupola`, `mansard`, `balconyBlock`, `portalBase`) builds Buenos Aires–style blocks on it.

**Architecture:** The pure geometry lives in `src/kit/forms/porteno.ts`: `clipToPoly`, `clipConvex`, `clipHalf`, `chamferPoly`, `polyFootprint` and `frustum`, together with the four new recipes. Each existing recipe gets an `if (lot.poly)` branch that clips its bodies to `polyFootprint(lot, 0, 2, "oblique")` (or clips its own body rect and chamfers it). With no `poly`, the code path is byte-identical to today's, and a hash snapshot taken before any change pins that. The test oracles for parcels (wedge lots, inside-polygon, corner cut) live in `src/kit/lot-fixtures.ts`, shared by `porteno.test.ts` and `kit.test.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest, Pixi v8 (rendering only; nothing here touches Pixi).

**Spec:** issue #476 (body), `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-resume-centro-costero-design.md` §6.2, the decisions D3/D11 in #471, and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-resume-nivel-2-kit-zonificacion-luces-design.md` §5 (kit contract).

## Technical decisions (declared; the issue leaves them open or is inconsistent)

- **T1. Chamfer rule.** The issue asks to port the plano's `chamfer` (cut where `|cos| < 0.35`, both edges `> 3k`), and also asks for chamfered *oblique* corners. On the issue's own wedge (`x + 0.42·y = c`), the oblique vertices are at 113° (`|cos|` 0.39) and 157° (`|cos|` 0.92), so the plano rule would leave both uncut. `chamferPoly(poly, k, corners = "all")` therefore cuts `k` along both edges at every vertex whose two edges are `> 3k` and that is not nearly flat (`cos > −FLAT_COS`, `FLAT_COS = 0.95`). `corners: "oblique"` also skips the near-right vertices (`|cos| < RIGHT_COS = 0.35`, the plano's ochava class). The plano's cuts are a subset of `"all"`.
- **T2. "Corner cut ≥ 2"** is tested as: for each oblique vertex `V` of `lot.poly` (`|cos| > 1e-6`), with unit edge directions `e1`, `e2`, no ground-level plan polygon overlaps the triangle `(V, V + 2·e1, V + 2·e2)` by more than 1e-6 of area. The literal reading ("no footprint vertex within 2 of `V`") fails for any body set back from the lot edges, whose clipped vertex falls about 1 from `V` even after a correct 2 cut. The triangle is what a 2 chamfer removes.
- **T3. Ground-level solid**: every `piece.ground` solid, plus every solid whose `bounds.min.z ≤ lot.z + 1e-6`. Its plan polygons are the footprint for `poly`, each triangle for `ground`, and the box corners for everything else.
- **T4. `cornerCupola` height.** The issue gives both "4..6 floors" and "h 5.5..8.5", which disagree at `floorH` 3. The body height is drawn in 5.5..8.5 (the casco's 8.5 cap in D12), capped so the cupola fits under `recipeCap`, and the floor count is `floorsFor(h)`. The per-floor cornices are stacked: each floor is its own body, `fh − 0.15` tall, with a 0.15 trim slab on top that overhangs 0.25. That way no thin cornice crosses a body (the interpenetration oracle is box-based, and a body with a facade never interlocks).
- **T5. Mansard roof** is a `mesh` frustum (the footprint inset 0.8 at the base, 2 at the top, 2.2 tall), because `hipRoof` only takes rectangles. The dormers stand on the 0.8 ledge between the cornice and the frustum, on the axis-aligned south and east edges (`CAMERA_SIDES`), so their boxes only touch the frustum's.
- **T6. Vertical windows.** `Facade.window` holds fractions of the column width and floor height, so the issue's `{ w: 0.6, h: 1.4 }` is read as an absolute size. `portalBase` uses `cols = max(2, round(longest edge / 2))` and `window: { w: 0.3, h: 1.4 / style.floorH }`.
- **T7.** `ring` in `courtyard.ts` gets exported, for `cornerCupola`.

## Review Focus

1. A `poly` that leaves only a sliver of a body (clip area ≈ 0) → no solid with fewer than 3 vertices or zero area, and nothing throws. The test goes in Task 3 (`a sliver wedge yields no degenerate poly`).
2. A non-convex `poly` → clipped against its convex hull, as the issue says. The test goes in Task 2.
3. `poly` given clockwise or counter-clockwise → the same clip. The test goes in Task 2.
4. `lot.open` empty → the cupola goes southeast, the portal south and the balconies south, with no throw. The test goes in Task 5/6 (`with no open side`).
5. A cut on the far, northwest corner, not only the camera-facing one → still inside the polygon and chamfered. `WEDGE_LOTS` includes an NW cut, used by the parcel tests (Task 1 fixture, exercised in Tasks 3–6).

### Task 2: Polygon helpers

**Files:** Create `src/kit/forms/porteno.ts` (helpers only) and its test section in `porteno.test.ts`.

**Interfaces (produces):**
- `clipConvex(subject: Vec2[], clip: Vec2[]): Vec2[]` is Sutherland–Hodgman against every edge of `clip`. If `clip` is not convex, it clips against `convexHull(clip)`. It works with either winding, drops consecutive duplicates (1e-9), returns `[]` below 3 vertices or at area < 1e-9, and keeps the subject's winding.
- `clipToPoly(rect: Rect, poly: Vec2[]): Vec2[]` = `clipConvex(rect corners in canonical order, poly)`.
- `clipHalf(poly: Vec2[], a: number, b: number, c: number): Vec2[]` keeps `a·x + b·y ≤ c`.
- `chamferPoly(poly: Vec2[], k: number, corners: "all" | "oblique" = "all"): Vec2[]` follows T1 (`RIGHT_COS 0.35`, `FLAT_COS 0.95`).
- `polyFootprint(lot: Lot, inset: number, chamfer: number, corners: "all" | "oblique" = "all"): Vec2[]` = `chamferPoly(lot.poly ? clipToPoly(insetRect, lot.poly) : rect corners, chamfer, corners)`.
- `polyArea(poly: Vec2[]): number` (absolute) and `polyCentroid(poly: Vec2[]): Vec2`.

- [ ] **Step 1: tests** (`describe("polygon helpers")`):
  - `clipToPoly({0,0,24,18}, wedgeLot(L1,9).poly)` is a pentagon with area `24·18 − 9·3.78/2`;
  - a rect fully inside returns its 4 corners;
  - a rect fully outside returns `[]`;
  - the same clip with `poly` reversed has the same area (Review Focus 3);
  - a non-convex `poly` (an L) clips like its convex hull (Review Focus 2);
  - `chamferPoly` of the square 10×10 with k 2 is an octagon whose vertices are each exactly 2 from the nearest original corner, along an edge;
  - with k 4 (edges 10 ≤ 12) the square is unchanged;
  - `chamferPoly(square, 2, "oblique")` is unchanged;
  - on the wedge pentagon, `"oblique"` adds exactly 2 vertices per oblique corner (7 vertices in all) and leaves the three right corners;
  - `polyFootprint({...L1, poly: undefined}, 1, 2, "oblique")` is the 22×16 rect.

The topper goes at the tower centroid, and the south sign only if the tower rect lies wholly inside the podium.
- `decoStepped`: each tier is `chamferPoly(clipConvex(chamferedRect(...), poly), 2, "oblique")`; a tier with area < 4 ends the stack. The spire goes at the centroid of the last tier.
