# Hotel and the RESUME sign on its facade (cap 12): the tower and the helipad go, zone link, animated rooms (#474) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** the office tower, its crown and its helipad leave Resume; a 1970s hotel stands on `HOTEL_LOT` (classic three-floor base with chamfers and a portal toward the plaza, a 64 × 8 slab along `(1, −1)/√2` up to z 51) and carries the RESUME marquee sign on the slab's southeast face at cap 12. The zone link, the lab, the animation (rooms instead of the lit floor, papers along the facade) and the docs that pin the sign's site follow it.

**Architecture:** a new pure module `src/scenes/sign-hotel.ts` replaces `src/scenes/sign-crown.ts`: constants of the slab and the sign, `RESUME_SIGN` (same name and same `id: "cartel-torre"`), `SIGN_SITE`, `SIGN_PANEL`, `hotel(out, accents, lights, at?)` returning `HotelLights` and `hotelStandIn`. `city()` builds the hotel on the `HOTEL_LOT` parcel; `fixed.ts` keeps only the plaza (`plazaMayor`). The tower constants (`TOWER`, `TOWER_H`, `TOWER_FLOORS`, `LIT_FLOOR`, `TOWER_ISLAND`) and the lit-floor zoning rule disappear; `maxHeightFor` is re-aimed at the sign panel. `city-anim` animates `scene.hotel.rooms`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest (node, no Pixi), PixiJS v8 only for the screenshots (agent-browser).

**Spec:** issue #474 (binding: "Qué construir", "Quién cambia", "Decisiones que hereda este hijo", "Criterios de aceptación"); story #471 plan comment (D9, D10, D15); `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-resume-centro-costero-design.md` §7.

## Technical assumptions (declared, sourced where possible)

- **A1. `facade` on the slab poly.** `withPlates` (`src/iso/solids.ts`) paints a facade on every wall, but the `±U` end walls are edge-on in this projection, and the NW face is culled, so a `Facade` on the slab `poly` shows only on the southeast face. The end walls are blind in practice; no new facade option is added.
- **A2. Rooms.** `rooms` = `windowPatches(se, facade, floor)` for every floor of the SE wall from `visibleWalls(slab)`, keeping windows whose max z < `SIGN_Z0 − 0.5` or min z > `48.3 + 0.5`, as `poly` accents `color: "amber"`. With 12 floors on z 10..51 that is floors 0..5 (16 × 6 = 96 rooms). The issue names `facadeAccents`, which only emits `litFloor`; `windowPatches` per floor is the same geometry.
- **A3. Paper window.** `paperWindow` = centroid of the easternmost window of floor 4 (index 4 from the bottom), **moved 0.6 along `N`** (just in front of the glass), so the paper-never-enters-the-slab criterion (slab footprint grown 0.5) holds from frame 0. Without the offset the window centroid itself is inside the grown footprint.
- **A4. Rooms state in `createCityAnim`.** The initial 0.4 draw and the toggles use `blinkRng` (the stream the old lit floor used), drawn right after `blinkRng` is created, so the draws of the shared `rng` (papers, signs, windows, flicker) do not move. A toggle that would take the lit count out of `initial ± 5` toggles a room of the other state instead (the windows' band rule).
- **A5. `maxHeightFor(parcel, top)`.** Returns `FRONT_CAP_H` (10) when the box `rect × [0, MAX_BUILDING_H + PLINTH_H]` overlaps `screenBounds(bounds(SIGN_PANEL))` on screen and `isBehind(panelBounds, box)`, else `top`. `assign` uses `Math.min(top, maxHeightFor(p, top))` for parcels; the blocks (strip and east bank) keep `top`. `MAX_DISTRICT_H`, `maxDistrictHeight` and the old block-based `maxHeightFor` go.
- **A6. Hotel plinth.** The `HOTEL_LOT` parcel keeps its paving plinth (`parcelPlinth`); the base is `HOTEL_LOT` minus `SIDEWALK` on each side with 2.4 chamfers, z 0..10.
- **A7. `hotel` signature.** `hotel(out: Solid[], accents: Accent[], lights: FixedLight[], at: Vec3 = v3(0, 0, 0)): HotelLights` where `at` is a displacement from the real site; `lights` receives two lamps flanking the east portal (`placeLit` + `lamp` of `src/kit/props.ts`). `hotelStandIn(out, accents)` builds base and slab displaced by `−(center, 0)` (slab centered at the origin), no facade.

- **A8. Panel against the face.** The panel's back face is coplanar with the slab's SE face (`n = 4`, as the issue writes: "`n` de `thick/2` a `thick/2 + PANEL_T`"). The panel is a separate solid in front of the slab on the `N` axis, so `sortByDepth` paints it after the slab; a `sign-hotel.test.ts` case asserts the order with `sortByDepth`, and the captures (Task 8) confirm it. If the capture shows the slab over the panel, the panel's solids get `interlocks` with the slab or the gap rule of the old crown (0.06 behind the letters) is kept and the back face moved to `n = 4.01` — a 0.01 offset that no criterion measures.
- **A9. Cap floor.** Cap 12 is the floor of spec 2026-11-22 §7.3 ("Si el oráculo pide margen, se baja el cap (piso: `12`)") and the issue measured `checkSign = []` at 12. If `src/lab/signs.test.ts` is red at cap 12, that is the "se vuelve a consultar" case of §7.4: the run stops with a comment on the issue, never a known-red test and never a cap below 12.
- **A10. Intermediate states.** Tasks 2..6b land on the branch `agent/474-*`, never on `BASE` one by one: the PR squashes them. The temporary `lit()` of Task 2 (every room lit) lives only between two commits of this branch.

- **A11. `STATION_LAND` on screen (stage 3).** The issue's test compares the projected box of `STATION_LAND` with each solid's `screenBounds`. Measured on `main` of the story: one solid already fails that box test, a bars roof of `#472` at x 143..148, y 175..191, z ≤ 11.3, north of the plaza; it lies in the box but off the projected land (the land projects to a parallelogram). The eval compares the projected land (`screenHull`) with each solid's `screenHull` (`hullsOverlap`, `src/iso/depth.ts:29,50`): the rule of spec 2026-11-22 §7.4 ("ningún edificio de Resume tapa en pantalla la estación") without the box's false positives.
- **A12. 50×900.** `ZONE_SIZES` holds 50×900, narrower than the word (≈ 86 screen u) at any zone scale; the `zoneView("cv")` framing evals skip it. Measured with the new sign box: every other `ZONE_SIZES` host shows the sign whole with ≥ 8 px. Cover visibility on the phones: 27.7 % (430×932), 48.4 % (390×750), 36.5 % (360×740), matching the story's 28 / 48 / 37.
- **A13. Zone URL.** The Resume zone opens at `/area/resume/` (`src/router.test.ts:9`), the page the issue calls `/area/cv/`.
