> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the failed helipad signs A/B with one Resume sign: a dark crown panel with green filled channel letters on the real office tower (`CROWN_SITE`), with the helipad on top. ## Decisions (technical assumptions, all measured on the prototype)

Notation: `N = (1, 1, 0)/√2` (toward the camera), `U = (1, −1, 0)/√2` (along the sign), `W = measureText("Resume", 10.3).width`, `L = W + 2·3`. Every z is relative to `at.z` (the rooftop, 30 at the site).

1. **Glyph `m` (lettering.ts).** At `SIGN_FONT` the arches' flat tops, 0.5 grid long, give quads with a 0.736 u side. That is 1.22 px at 1.17, so level 2 `stroke` fails (≥ 2 px). The arches become `line(0, 4, 0.75, 5, 1.75, 5, 2.5, 4, 2.5, 0)` and `line(2.5, 4, 3.25, 5, 4.25, 5, 5, 4, 5, 0)`: the flat top is 1 grid long, like `u`'s bottom. Advance and counters do not change. The issue allows adjusting glyphs in `lettering.ts` as long as `lettering.test.ts` and `legibility.test.ts` stay green.
2. **Letter plane:** `pp = at + 4·N` (= `at + 2.83·(1, 1, 0)`). `RESUME_SIGN.plane = diagonalWall(pp − U·W/2 at z = baseline)`, so the word is centered on `pp`.
3. **Panel z (deviation).** The issue puts its bottom at `+2.4`. But the panel's back face, 0.06 + 0.6 behind `pp`, cuts the NE corner of the real machine room (x 126..132, y 225..229, top `+2.9`): the corner has `x + y = 261`, and the back face sits at `x + y ≈ 260.72`. So the panel starts at **`PANEL_Z0 = +3.2`** (machine room top `+2.9` plus the 0.3 lower beam under it). Baseline `= PANEL_Z0 + 2.7`, panel top `PANEL_Z1 = baseline + 10.3 + 2.7` (= 48.9 at the site). The margins (2.7 = 2 w) are the issue's.
4. **Panel:** `poly` `officeDark`. Footprint: a rectangle along `U` over `±L/2` around `pp`, from `−0.06` to `−0.66` along `N` (its SE face is 0.06 behind the letters). `z = PANEL_Z0`, `h = PANEL_Z1 − PANEL_Z0`. `RESUME_SIGN.backing = [panel at CROWN_SITE]`. `towerCrown` pushes a copy of it (the way `sign-platform.ts` copies its footprint).
5. **Letters:** for each quad of `RESUME_SIGN.glyphs[*].quads` (style `fill`), one accent `{ kind: "poly", pts, color: "green", alpha: 1 }`. Translate it by `at − CROWN_SITE` when `at ≠ CROWN_SITE`. No halo: level 3 passed without one (contrast 11.8 : 1, halo 0.07, OCR `Resume` at both scales).
6. **Structure (steel, all `interlock`ed):**
   - Three posts, 0.3², centered on the line `pp + N·(−0.66 − 0.15·√2)` (just behind the panel, touching its back face) at `t = −5, 0, +5` along `U`. Each runs from the cornice top `+0.4` up to the **slab underside** (not just the panel top: the slab rests on them).
   - Two beams, 0.3², along `U` over `±L/2`, centered at `N·(−0.36)` (the panel's mid-thickness): the lower one centered at z `PANEL_Z0 − 0.15`, the upper one at `PANEL_Z1 + 0.15`.
   - Two tensors, 0.2², one from each end of the lower beam to the nearest cornice corner, `(at.x ± 8.5, at.y ± 7.5)` clamped to the cornice (that is the nearest point of the cornice's edge). The end rests on the cornice top: the tensor's lowest point is `at.z + 0.4`, exact within 1e-6 (iterate the tilt to a fixed point, as `sign-helipad.ts` did).
   - Two NW pillars, 0.6², centered at `at + (−5, −1)` and `at + (−1, −5)`, from `+0.4` up to the slab underside. Both are clear of the machine room and of the antenna.
7. **Helipad (radius and height chosen, as the issue asks):** a 16-sided concrete `cylinder` centered at `at`, `r = 7`, `h = 0.5`, with its underside at **`SLAB_Z = PANEL_Z1 + 1.4`**. The slab's bottom edge overhangs `pp` by up to 3 u. A dense check (screen silhouette of the slab against the band 1 w above the ink, every 0.05 u along the word) gives a clearance of +0.56 z with these numbers. With `+0.4` it was −0.44, that is, encroaching. The oracle's 7 × 7 sampling misses that encroachment, so Task 2 pins it with a dense test.
   - On the slab (`PAD = SLAB_Z + 0.5`): a whitewash disc `cylinder` with `r = 5.5`, `h = 0.05` and 16 sides. An "H" of three whitewash prisms with `h = 0.06` on the disc (`PAD + 0.05`), centered at `at`: two 0.8 × 4 stems and a 2.4 × 0.8 bar.
   - 16 `dot` `greenMid` with `r = 0.25` at radius 6.4, every 22.5° starting from 0°, at z `PAD`.
   - A net of 16 steel beams with `w = 0.12` and `h = 0.6`, joining consecutive points at radius `7 − 0.06`, centered at z `PAD + 0.3`.
   - Two `industrialLamp`s with `h = 1` and `center: true` at radius 6.2, at 205° and 245° (the NW edge), z `PAD`, with their bulbs recolored `amber`.
8. **Interpenetration oracle (deviation).** The issue asks for the AABB oracle of `kit.test.ts` "copied". But the diagonal panel's AABB covers the whole rooftop, so it collides with the posts, the pillars and the antenna even though it touches none of them. The copy in `sign-crown.test.ts` therefore keeps the AABB filter and adds a second condition: the pair is flagged only if the plan convex hulls of their tessellated points also overlap (`hullsOverlap` from `src/iso/depth.ts` over `convexHull` from `src/iso/geometry.ts`). The interlock exception stays. This is exact for vertical solids and conservative for tilted beams.
9. **`towerStandIn(out, accents)`** puts the same geometry as `fixed.ts` at the origin. Tower: prism `(−8, −7, 0)`, 16 × 14 × 30, `officeDark`, no facade. Cornice: `(−8.5, −7.5, 30)`, 17 × 15 × 0.4, `office`. Machine room: `(−3, −2, 30.4)`, 6 × 4 × 2.5, `officeDark`. Antenna: `cylinder` `steel` at `(0, 0, 32.9)`, `r = 0.3`, `h = 5`, `sides: 4`. No accents.
10. **Exhibits:** `cartel-torre` and `cartel-torre-escena` take the slot of `cartel-helipuerto(-b)` (right after `selva`).
    - `#cartel-torre-escena` uses the issue's `box { x0: 108, y0: 206, x1: 150, y1: 248 }`: the crown's solids span x 113.6..149.5 and y 211.6..247.5.
    - Its `h` is the smallest integer that passes the framing and neighbor tests of `exhibits.test.ts`. The prototype passed with 53. Measure it and write the measurement in a comment, as `plataforma-escena` does.
11. **`docs.test.ts`.** Its README test asserts that the four #352 exhibit ids exist, so it would fail once the helipad exhibits are gone. The README itself is closed by #380 (the issue says so). The existence assertion skips the two retired ids, with a comment naming #378 and #380. The README text check stays.
12. **`world-baseline.test.ts`.** Drop the two `cartel-helipuerto` hashes and record `cartel-torre`, with a `// #378:` line.
13. **Known effect, stated in the PR (not a failure).** The real antenna's pulsing `amberMid` light is an accent, and accents are drawn over every solid. It shows faintly between the "s" and the "u". Level 3 passes with it (frame 0, `?speed=0`), and the world does not change in this story.

## Assumptions

- **Panel bottom +3.2 instead of +2.4.** Two issue sentences conflict: "de z `at.z + 2.4`" and "ningún sólido de la corona interpenetra … a los de la torre real". The machine room (`src/city/fixed.ts:38`, top at +2.9) makes them incompatible, and the hard constraint wins. Rejected: thinning the panel to 0.3 (the issue fixes 0.6) or moving the plane further SE (the issue fixes 4 u).
- **Interpenetration oracle = AABB filter + plan-hull SAT.** `bounds()` is an AABB (`src/iso/solids.ts:452`), so the copied oracle would flag the diagonal panel against every rooftop solid. Rejected: excusing the panel by interlock, because the issue says interlock goes only on steel.
- **Slab r 7 with its underside at panel top +1.4, and posts up to the slab.** The issue gives the radius and the height ("elegir su radio y su cota") but says "+0.4". The dense test pins the clearance. Rejected: r ≤ 6.1 at +0.4, which no longer reaches the posts at t = ±5 (5.9 u from the center).
- **Glyph `m` changed** to pass the level-2 stroke rule. The issue allows glyph changes for OCR; here they are needed for level 2. Only "Resume" uses `m` (the words in `src/scenes/sign-*.ts`), so no other hash or `REPORT.md` row changes.
- **Level-3 knobs, if Task 5 fails:** the issue's own list ("se ajusta este cartel (alpha, halo, tono del panel)"; glyphs for OCR). Every fix goes in as new commits on the branch; nothing is rewritten.
- **Photos:** if Commons gives fewer than 4 usable free photos, broaden the search terms (skyline crown, rooftop sign night, office helipad). The criterion stays 4–8.
