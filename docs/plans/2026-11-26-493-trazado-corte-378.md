# #493 Trazado: plaza y hotel estirados, Diagonal Sur y Avenida del Sur al sur, corte en y 378, Bulevar y Costanera hasta el borde — plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Move the plaza, the hotel lot, the Diagonal Sur and the Avenida del Sur 30 u south, give the city one more row of blocks (y 348..378, barrio clásico), cut city and coastal neighbourhood at a single constant (y 378), and run the Bulevar Central and the Costanera to the south edge of the world (y 714) with their furniture.

**Architecture:** `src/scenes/city-plan.ts` gets the new constants and `COASTAL_CUT_Y = 378`. Two bounds with two jobs: `CITY_BOUNDS` (y1 348, the Avenida del Sur) stays the domain of the city's *streets* (axis lanes, crossings, ordinary streets, `inDomain`), and `PLAN_BOUNDS` (y1 `COASTAL_CUT_Y`) becomes the domain of the parcels the city *builds*. Every other `318` that meant "city vs coastal" reads `COASTAL_CUT_Y`. South of the content, a narrow asphalt corridor under the two N–S axes (`builtAt`) carries them past the urban reach to the bleed edge. `southStreets` exempts the boulevard band from the cover cut, and the waterfront runs to the bleed edge.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, vitest, PixiJS v8 (screenshots and FPS only), agent-browser.

**Spec:** issue #493 (binding) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-resume-hotel-cartel-2-5d-design.md` §3, §4, §5.

## Assumptions (technical, declared)

- **A1. Two bounds.**
  - The issue asks for both `CITY_BOUNDS.y1 = 348` and "replace the `318`s (CITY_BOUNDS among them) with one constant (378)". Both hold only if `CITY_BOUNDS` and the parcel cut have different jobs.
  - `CITY_BOUNDS` (y1 348) is the city's street domain. The city owns the crossings on the y 348 avenue, and `southStreets` paints from 348 southward, as today at 318.
  - `PLAN_BOUNDS` (y1 `COASTAL_CUT_Y`) is the domain of the parcels the city builds, so it covers the new row.
  - `inParcel`'s default list becomes `parcels(PLAN_BOUNDS)`, so that furniture also avoids the new row.
- **A2. Fixed pieces move with the plaza.** The civic palace and the theatre go from row 291 to row 321, the parcels that now face the plaza's south edge. The auditorium (13, 351) becomes city land and moves to cell (43, 381), a 24 × 22 parcel south of the Avenida del Barrio. If it is not a south parcel at least 20 × 20, the implementer takes the nearest south parcel that is, and records which.
- **A3. The corridor.**
  - South of `WORLD.y1`, `builtAt` returns `"urban"` in every 18 u bleed cell that a N–S axis band crosses. Water still wins.
  - These are the cells x −6..30 (boulevard) and 174..192 (Costanera), down to the bleed edge.
  - The axes end at `WORLD.y1 + BLEED.y` (714).
  - A road through the jungle reads as a road leaving the city. Widening the whole urban reach would leave empty asphalt, which is ticket #496's job to fill.
- **A4.** `barrioAt` keeps `if (c.y > 380) b = "residencial"` and the `moderno → transicion` rule at y 290. The towers' south limit (290 → 320) is §7, ticket #495.
- **A5.** `CITY_EDGE.south` (324) is a terrain classification (a multiple of `CELL`), not the city / coastal cut, so it stays where it is.
