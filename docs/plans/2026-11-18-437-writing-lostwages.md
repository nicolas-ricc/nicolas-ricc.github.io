# WRITING in LostWages, no dark panel (#437) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Writing platform's sign says `WRITING`, in capitals, in LostWages (cyan neon, no dark backing panel). The platform stays (deck, piles, railing, ladder, hut, mast), and so do the frame beams and posts. Every legibility guardrail passes without being loosened.

**Architecture:** `scripts/marquee-word.py` composes the word from the TTF, which is not committed. It gains a closing step that fills the font's double-tube slits. `src/kit/lostwages-writing.ts` replaces `src/kit/chancery-writing.ts`, and `LOSTWAGES_FACE` replaces `CHANCERY_FACE`/`CHANCERY_X`. `sign-platform.ts` drops the panel: the spec's `backing` becomes what really stands behind the letters (the frame posts and the sea surface they project onto). The frame and deck re-derive from `FRAME.L`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vitest, Python 3 + fontTools + shapely (generator only), agent-browser + tesseract.js (level 3, `npm run legibility`).

**Spec:** issue #437. The rules it must keep are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-carteles-de-zona-rediseno-design.md` §4 (levels 1–3) and §8.2 (the "Writing en Black Chancery" entry is the template for the new one).

## Global Constraints

- The sign reads `WRITING` in LostWages (Nick Curtis, 1001Fonts Free For Commercial Use), cyan, all capitals. Download: https://www.1001fonts.com/download/lostwages.zip. Page: https://www.1001fonts.com/lostwages-font.html.
- No TTF in the repo. Only the composed word, generated by `scripts/marquee-word.py`, whose docstring carries the exact command and the URL.
- The 1001Fonts EULA (§3) forbids modifying, altering or adapting the font. Weight (erode/dilate) and slit closing are applied to the rendered word, as the script already does for CNRGN Builder. Redrawing glyphs is out.
- `FONT_RULES`, `SIGN_RULES` and the level-3 thresholds are not touched. If the font is too thin or too thick, the script adjusts it, not the rules.
- The platform stays: deck, piles, railing, ladder, hut, mast. Only the `officeDark` panel goes. The frame (two beams, posts) stays and holds the neon.
- Code, comments and test names in English; exhibit ids stay Spanish (`src/english.test.ts`). Specs stay in Spanish.

## Measured in a spike (2026-11-18, reverted; values to reuse)

- LostWages draws each stroke as a tube outline with a slit counter ≈ 0.02 cap wide down its middle. As it comes, `checkLayouts` fails on those counters, and the letters touch through their baseline bars (overlap).
- A morphological closing of 0.015 cap fills every slit. Only the loops of `W`, `I`, `T`, `I` stay as counters, ⌀ 0.174 cap before weight.
- Pure dilation cannot replace the closing. Below 0.015 the slit remnants stay (letters split in pieces, or counters < 0.03 w). At 0.015 the loop counters drop to 1.01 w.
- Chosen parameters: thin `-0.0125` (dilate 0.0125 cap per side), air `0.145`, close `0.015`. That gives a face of 0.138 cap (stems of W, R, N, G; I and T 0.1365), gaps of 1.05 w, rhythm 1.00, counters 1.08 w, ink width 7.095 cap, and ink y from −0.174 to 1.012 cap.
- `WRITING_CAP` 9.4. The phone stroke is the binding rule: 0.138 · 9.4 · 1.4 · 0.83 = 1.507 px ≥ 1.5. The x-height is the cap: 15.4 px at 1.17.
- Ink width 66.7 u (Black Chancery: 59.54). With `SIGN_MARGIN` 3 and `END` 6, the tall site's north-east deck corner reaches the outbound lane: `sea-anim.test.ts` "no point of the lane ends up behind a static solid" fails at d = 384..388. Moving `PLATFORM_SITES.tall` instead breaks `responsive.test.ts` "1180×820: Writing's word center is low and right" (cx < 0.52) for every shift that clears the lane, so the sites stay. The fix is `SIGN_MARGIN` 1.5 (the frame no longer has to hold a panel's margin) and `END` 4 (the hut spans 0.52..3.48 u from the deck end, which clears the end railing). Then `DECK_HALF` = 38.85, and the gate passes.
- With `backing` = a sea surface patch, `src/lab/signs.test.ts` (levels 1 and 2 at both sites, against the crop and against the whole world) passes.
- **Level 3 fails on OCR.** `npm run legibility -- --only plataforma-escena` gave contrast 12.48:1 and 10.53:1 ✓ and halo 0.05 and 0.03 ✓, but OCR `WRETENG` (1.17) and `WREFENG` (0.83) ✗. LostWages' capital `I` (a stem with a bottom loop and a baseline bar) reads as `E`. With the dotless `ı` instead, OCR gave `WBRLILNG` and `WREFLNG`: `R` and `T` fail too. The letterforms fail, not the weight. See "Open question".

## Open question (blocking, for the issue author)

§4.3 point 4 requires OCR to return the exact word at 1.17, with a fixed preprocess shared by all signs. The acceptance asks for the guardrails to pass without loosening them. The EULA forbids altering the glyphs. These three cannot all hold with LostWages' `WRITING`. Options:

1. Accept that level 3 OCR fails for this sign: record it as a documented exception in §8.2 and REPORT.md. That changes the docs gate ("no ✗") and the OCR rule for this sign.
2. Change the OCR floor for display fonts (for example a human-read check instead of tesseract for this sign).
3. Choose another font, or another wording or case, that passes OCR.
4. Get written permission from the author to adapt the `I`, `R` and `T` glyphs (the EULA's §3).

Tasks 1–3 below are the same under options 1, 2 and 4. Task 4 (level 3 and docs) depends on the answer.

**Answered 2026-11-19 by the author (issue comment): option 1.** See "Resolution" at the end.

## Review Focus

1. A letter split into pieces by the closing or the weight must stop the generator (`SystemExit`) instead of silently dropping a piece. The script raises "close: a letter split in pieces"; Task 1 runs it on the real font.
2. The sea behind the letters must really be water at both sites, not land or foam: the backing patch is checked against the terrain (`waterCellMat` non-null over the patch, at z `WATER_Z`). Task 2 test.
3. Without the panel, the beams must still clear the ink by `PAD` 1.6 above and below, the loops included (ink y0 is −0.174 cap). Task 2 test.
4. A consumer that mutates the platform layer's posts must not reach `BLOG_SIGNS[site].backing`: copies, `toEqual` but `not.toBe`. Task 2 test.
5. Contrast against the lightest tone the sea patch's material can take (`glow`), not just `top`, stays ≥ 4.5 : 1, so the terrain's tone jitter cannot drop it below the rule. Task 2 test.

## Resolution (2026-11-19)

The author answered the blocking question on the issue: **option 1**.

- Level 3 OCR: a documented exception for this sign only. The normal capital `I` stays (not the dotless `ı`); the expected OCR ✗ is `WRETENG` / `WREFENG`.
- It is written down in spec 2026-11-15 §8.2 (deviations) and in `REPORT.md`, with the measured readings, the reason (the letterforms, not the weight) and a reference to the EULA's §3.
- The docs gate ("no ✗") changes **only for `plataforma-escena*` and only for OCR**. Contrast and halo stay ✓; levels 1 and 2 (`checkLayouts`, `checkSign` at both sites) are not loosened. No other sign inherits the exception.
- The morphological closing is accepted under the same criterion as CNRGN Builder's erosion: a treatment of the rasterised outline done by `marquee-word.py`; the font file is neither modified nor redistributed. §8.2 records it next to the erosion precedent.

Decisions taken on resume (technical assumptions):

- Q11: the stroke the oracle checks is the thinnest stem, not the dominant one. If the generated word's thinnest stem (I, T) is under `LOSTWAGES_FACE`, `thin` grows until it is not (adjusting the script, not the rules), and `WRITING_CAP` is re-derived so the phone stroke stays ≥ 1.5 px.
- Q17: the `marquee.ts` constant swap moves into Task 2, so every commit typechecks.
- Task 4: `REPORT.md` and the docs gate accept the OCR ✗ for `plataforma-escena*` only, as above.

Measured on resume (fontTools 4.46.0, shapely 2.0.3, Python 3.12.3): with thin −0.0125 the I and T stems are 0.1365 cap (horizontal section at y 0.5), under the 0.138 face. Ruling (Q11): thin **−0.0133**. Then every stem is ≥ 0.1381 (I, T; W 0.1395). `LOSTWAGES_FACE` stays 0.138 and `WRITING_CAP` stays 9.4. Gaps are 1.05 w, counters 1.07 w, ink width 7.1065 cap (66.80 u), ink y −0.1748..1.0132 cap, and `checkLayouts` = []. The command in Task 1 becomes `... -0.0133 0.145 0.015`.
