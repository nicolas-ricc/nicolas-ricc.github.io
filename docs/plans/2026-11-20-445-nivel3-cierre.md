## Technical decisions (declared)

1. **`WRITING_CAP` 10.8 → 11.5.** Baseline run on this branch (2026-11-20): contrast 9.85–12.90:1 and halo 0.13–0.15 everywhere, OCR `WRITING` at 1.17, but `WRITHG` at 0.83 at both sites (tesseract merges `IN` into `H`). The issue's two knobs were tried and do not fix it (board `glass`, paint alpha 0.9, both: still `WRITHG`, contrast ≥ 11.5). The issue only lists those knobs for contrast below 7:1; the story #441 gives the rule for anything else: «si algo no pasa, se ajusta la tabla, la pintura o la palabra con el script» and «No bajar la caja alta sin re-medir». The child forbids touching the word (the glyph file) and the guardrails, not the paint's size. Sweep, both sites, both scales: 10.8 and 11.2 fail the 0.83 OCR; 11.3, 11.4, 11.5, 11.6, 11.7 and 11.8 pass all four rows (contrast ≥ 8.87:1). 11.5 is the middle of the passing band (±0.2 margin). With 11.6 the whole suite's world rules (sites on water, lanes, ships across the cycle, level 2, budget) stayed green; only pins failed (the cap, the `plataforma` baseline hash, docs).
2. **Time-lapse clock.** `/lab/blog.html?bloom=on&speed=0.000001`: at that speed the page's own ticks advance the platform clock by about 1e-4 ms per frame, so it starts at 0. The app ticker's listener is hooked as in CLAUDE.md's time-lapse method (patch `Ticker.prototype.update` for a moment, keep the ticker with `_minElapsedMS`, take `_head.next._fn` and `_context`). Each pose is reached by calling the listener once with `{ deltaMS: (HEAVE.periodMs / 4) / 0.000001 }`: `stage.tick` receives exactly `periodMs/4` = 1675 ms. Then `renderer.render(stage)`, and the screenshot. Pose 1 is taken before any call.
3. **The aspect threshold** for the §8.2 entry is computed as `docs.test.ts` did in #442: the smallest integer `w ≥ 900` with `writingSite(w, 900) === "wide"`, as `(w/900).toFixed(2)` with a comma. It is 1,53 at cap 10.8. The new block pins the value computed live.
4. **The plan size of the board** in the entry and in 2026-11-15 §8.3 comes from `PLATFORM_DECK` (side lengths, one decimal), live in the test.
5. **`src/english.test.ts`**: the whole `excepción|"erosión"|"cierre"` entry of `src/lab/docs.test.ts` is removed with its comment. After (a) none of the three is in that file.
